governed domain architectures

apex solar kiss is the builder practice. It builds governed domain architectures: systems that make a decision-owner's intent, constraints, references, and judgment explicit enough that generative work stays explainable, auditable, and answerable in its domain.

A deterministic governance architecture brackets the generative execution span. The governing rules and decision rights stay explicit while generation varies inside the bound, and human judgment stays at the apex rather than merely in the loop. The underlying primitive is bounded generativity: define the grammar, permit bounded variance, make selection and governance explicit.

The practice is one thing; the projects are several. The current portfolio includes two domain applications and the builder machine used to build and govern them. All of it is open source.

domain applications

Commercial visual production. It turns brand rules, creative direction, references, required outputs, and approval boundaries into a structured definition a production system can carry — a definition layer that sits above changing tools and models rather than inside any one of them.

repo >>

Public-planning implementation intelligence. It reads public data to reconstruct how civic commitments move from decision into delivery, showing progress, delay, change, and uncertainty rather than collapsing implementation into a single verdict — and recording what the evidence cannot show as an observability limit.
