ASK // apex solar kiss // design // bonAmi.ai

bonAmi.ai uses real-time AI to monitor users' interpersonal interactions — such as in-person conversations, phone calls, or digital communication — and detects early signs of conflict, emotional escalation, or socially inappropriate behavior. It offers discreet feedback to help users regulate their tone, language, and responses on the spot. When conversations reach a danger zone, the app can trigger subtle but powerful interventions — like a fake phone call, emergency text, or personalized overlay — to interrupt destructive patterns and offer a chance to reset. Whether you're navigating tense workplace discussions or emotional moments at home, bonAmi.ai is a steady, invisible partner in fostering empathy, calm, and constructive communication.

Real-Time Emotional & Conflict Detection

AI continuously monitors the user's speech, tone, and interaction dynamics to detect early signs of rising emotional tension, miscommunication, or potential antisocial behavior.

Discreet In-the-Moment Feedback

Subtle nudges through haptic cues, visual alerts, or whispered prompts (via earbuds) to help the user self-correct before the interaction deteriorates.

Escalation Protocols for Crisis Moments

When emotional or behavioral thresholds reach a danger zone—e.g., yelling, verbal aggression, or prolonged negative spirals—the app triggers creative interventions designed to break the pattern. Examples include:

  • Fake phone call: The app rings with a realistic incoming call screen, prompting the user to "step away." The AI voice can say something calming or redirecting once answered.
  • Emergency text notification: A realistic urgent alert (“Hey, I need to talk now”) breaks the flow and gives the user a socially acceptable way to pause.
  • On-screen “Reality Check” Overlay: A sudden UI overlay with personalized messaging based on prior patterns (e.g., “You promised yourself you wouldn’t raise your voice like this.”)

Post-Interaction Review & Growth Coaching

The app offers conversation summaries, tone graphs, flagged moments, and tailored tips for self-improvement based on what was detected during the exchange.

  2. Couples in Therapy or Working on Communication
  3. Adult Working on Impulse Control and Empathy
