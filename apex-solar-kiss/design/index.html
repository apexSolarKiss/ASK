Design architecture: the visual foundations shared across the ASK family — tokens, type, and the wordmark — plus the artifact-inheritance patterns and surface patterns built on them. Downstream surfaces use them without redefining them. A-S-K.studio vendors those foundations and the surface-shell pattern from design-system-ASK.

The overview, style guide and pattern previews are served from design-system-ASK's live deployment. They update from that owner surface rather than through a copy in this site.
