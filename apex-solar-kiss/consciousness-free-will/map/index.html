What the geometry does not claim. Radial distance encodes projection level only; sector width encodes corpus weight only, never importance and never authority; angular placement is compositional, and two sectors that touch carry no relation to each other. Governed edges are the only relation encoding on this surface.
Level of detail is not filtering. Zoom reveals concept names, then identifiers; all 548 primary objects stay on the map. A filter is explicit, bounds the map to a section, and reports its census. The 479 evidence owners open from atlas by exact identifier and never enter the graph.
