Yes, it can pulse — with two caveats worth your ruling. The pulse animates the OPACITY of a duplicate glowing layer, not the shadow itself: animating text-shadow repaints every glyph every frame, which on a page of links is a real mobile cost. Second, WCAG 2.2.2 covers content that moves or blinks automatically for more than five seconds, and a glow that never stops is squarely that. It is held static under prefers-reduced-motion here, which is the mitigation, but a permanently pulsing link on every surface is a bigger decision than a colour.
